HOW TO PLAY

🎲ROLL

Click the 3D dice (or the ROLL button) on your turn. Roll a 6 for +5 pts and a bonus roll.

🪜LADDERS

Land on a ladder base to climb to its top. Each climb awards +10 pts instantly.

🐍SNAKES

Land on a serpent's head and you slide down to its tail. Each bite costs −5 pts.

🏁WINNING

Reach cell 100 exactly — overshooting bounces back. Win +100 pts, loss −30 pts.

VIEWS

Top View is the default. Press 🧭 in-game for a 3D perspective view with rotate/zoom controls.

🤖BOTS

Easy blunders, Medium has Serpent Sense, Hard adds a Snake Shield and never slips.

🏆CUP

Tournament: first to 2 match wins vs bots or a local rival. Champion bonus +250 pts.
