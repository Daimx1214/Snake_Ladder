# 🐍 Snake & Ladder — Temple of a Thousand Steps 🪜

> 🎮 A premium, interactive Snake & Ladder experience with animated visuals, multiple game modes, AI opponents, local multiplayer, difficulty levels, and an immersive 3D board view.

## ✨ Overview

**Snake & Ladder — Temple of a Thousand Steps** is a browser-based Snake & Ladder game designed to provide a more polished and interactive experience than a traditional board game.

The project includes an animated splash screen, player/champion selection, multiple game modes, difficulty settings, local multiplayer, tournament gameplay, leaderboard, shop, settings, sound controls, fullscreen support, and an interactive 3D board experience.

The project was built with the help of **Qwen 3.8-Max** and focused heavily on creating an engaging UI/UX and smooth game experience.

---

## 🎮 Features

### 🐍 Core Gameplay

* 🎲 Interactive dice-based gameplay
* 🐍 Snake mechanics
* 🪜 Ladder mechanics
* 👥 Turn-based multiplayer gameplay
* 🏆 Win/loss tracking
* 📊 Player position tracking
* ⏱️ Turn timer
* ✨ Animated game feedback

### 🤖 Game Modes

#### 🤖 Play With Bot

Play against AI opponents with different difficulty levels:

* 🟢 Easy
* 🟡 Medium
* 🔴 Hard

#### 👥 Local Multiplayer

Play with friends on the same device.

* Supports **2–4 players**
* Pass-and-play gameplay

#### 🏆 Tournament

Compete in a tournament-style mode:

* Serpent Cup
* First to 2 wins

#### 🌐 Online Arena

An online multiplayer mode is included in the interface and planned for future development.

## 🎮 How to Play

1. 🚀 Launch the game.
2. ▶️ Press **Start**.
3. 👤 Enter your player name.
